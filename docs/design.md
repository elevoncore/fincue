## Brand & Style

The design system projects deliberate financial clarity, calm authority, and proactive intelligence. Built for individuals and high-earning households navigating complex personal cash flow, investments, and forward-looking budgets, the aesthetic rejects the loud, gamified tropes of legacy consumer fintech in favor of understated, institutional-grade precision reminiscent of modern infrastructure tools like Ramp, Mercury, and Linear.

### Visual Character

- **Restrained & Structural:** Structure is declared through razor-thin borders, disciplined grid rhythm, and precise typography rather than heavy fills or dramatic surface shifts.
- **Calm Authority:** Surfaces are predominantly crisp, neutral whites and slate-tinted canvas backdrops that recede, allowing numbers, status indicators, and proactive advisory cues to take primary focus.
- **Data-First Integrity:** Every financial metric relies on tabular numeric alignment and balanced contrast to facilitate immediate scanning without cognitive fatigue.
- **Anti-Trend Commitment:** No frosted glass blurs, skeuomorphic gloss, or saturated rainbow gradients. Visual interest comes from subtle state transitions, intentional micro-copy treatments, and razor-sharp component boundaries.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate departure from generic corporate blue by anchoring on deep, architectural cyan-teals paired with cool, crystalline slate neutrals. Semantic tones are precisely calibrated to ensure instant recognition of fiscal health without creating ambient alarm.

### Palette Architecture

- **Primary (`#0F766E` / Deep Teal):** Used for primary interactive actions, high-signal advisory highlights, focus rings, and active navigational states.
- **Secondary (`#0E7490` / Rich Cyan-Teal):** Employed for supplementary data visualizations, secondary progress indicators, and interactive chart paths.
- **Tertiary (`#0D5C58` / Dense Forest Teal):** Used for solid hover states, dark badge fills, and high-emphasis focal items.
- **Neutral Core (`#090A0C` to `#F8FAFC`):**
  - Surface Ground (Canvas): `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50)
  - Surface Raised (Cards): `#FFFFFF` (White)
  - Surface Sunken (Data tables, input tracks): `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100)
  - Border Subtlety: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) for standard card borders; `#CBD5E1` (Slate 300) for inputs and dividers
  - Text Primary: `#0F172A` (Slate 900)
  - Text Secondary: `#475569` (Slate 600)
  - Text Muted: `#94A3B8` (Slate 400)

### Status & Semantic System

- **Positive / On-Track (`#16A34A` / `#15803D`):** Surfaced for budget surpluses, positive net worth delta, and completed milestones. Background pill wash: `#F0FDF4`.
- **Warning / Near-Limit (`#D97706` / `#B45309`):** Reserved for spending velocity thresholds (80–99% of budget capacity). Background pill wash: `#FFFBEB`.
- **Critical / Over-Budget (`#DC2626` / `#B91C1C`):** Applied exclusively to account deficits, overdraft risks, and hard budget breaches. Background pill wash: `#FEF2F2`.

### Dark Mode Adaptation Logic

Dark mode reverses the luminance ladder while preserving hue identity: canvas transitions to `#090A0F`, raised cards to `#111318`, borders to `#1E222B`, and teal primary shifts slightly higher in value (`#14B8A6`) to preserve 4.5:1 contrast against dark slate surfaces.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes structural clarity and rapid vertical scannability across dense data screens.

### Font Roles

- **Geist (Headings & Interface Body):** Chosen for its mechanical neutrality, neutral aperture, and compact x-height. It feels engineered rather than decorative, keeping layout chrome invisible.
- **JetBrains Mono (Numerical Values & Financial Metrics):** Assigned to currency sums, tabular reports, percentages, timestamps, and advisory rate calculations. Enforces `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to guarantee vertical decimal and digit alignment across financial ledgers and dashboard KPI tiles.

### Hierarchy & Composition Rules

- Never use bold weights (`700+`) on display headers; cap header weights at `600` (SemiBold) with negative letter-tracking to maintain high-end editorial restraint.
- Micro-labels (`label-sm`) use subtle uppercase styling with relaxed tracking (`+0.03em`) for section delimiters and metric prefixes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored on an 8px base rhythm (with 4px micro-steps) tuned specifically for a 1440px desktop workbench with fluid scaling.

### Shell & Grid Structure

- **Persistent Left Navigation Rail:** 260px fixed width on standard desktop screens (>=1280px), collapsing into a 72px icon-only rail or an off-canvas drawer on tablet/mobile screens (<1024px).
- **Workspace Canvas:** Fluid right-hand pane with a maximum containment width of 1440px, centered on ultra-wide viewports.
- **Dashboard Metric Grid:** 12-column grid utilizing `1.25rem` (20px) gutters. Standard configurations:
  - Top KPI band: 4 equal cards (span-3 each).
  - Analytical breakdown: 8-column main chart/ledger paired with 4-column contextual advisor feed.
  - Side-by-side comparative views: 6-column split (span-6).

### Adaptive Breakpoints

- **Desktop (1280px–1440px+):** Full 260px sidebar visible; 3-column and 4-column dashboard cards active; `margin-desktop` (32px) exterior canvas margins.
- **Tablet (768px–1279px):** Sidebar converts to condensed icon dock (72px) or collapsible flyout. 12-column layouts reflow into stacked 2-column or full-width blocks; gutter drops to `1rem` (16px).
- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column stack. Sidebar transitions to an off-canvas sheet triggered by a top-bar hamburger. Metric cards collapse to 100% width with touch-swipable tabs for auxiliary charts.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy in this system relies on structured surface separation and razor-thin outlines rather than prominent drop shadows or blurring.

### Surface Tiers & Boundaries

- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC`. The foundational canvas behind all panels and navigation.
- **Tier 1 (Cards & Sidebars):** `#FFFFFF`. Foreground containers housing modules, tables, and analytics. Framed with a uniform `1px solid #E2E8F0` border.
- **Tier 2 (Popovers, Modals & Dropdowns):** `#FFFFFF`. Lifted slightly above the canvas with an ultra-diffused, cool-tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Context Menus & Tooltips):** `#0F172A` (Dark slate inverse) with crisp white micro-copy, providing maximum contrast against white cards without cluttering the page.

### Shadow Character

Shadows are strictly minimal. The standard card elevation (`shadow-sm`) is defined as `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Borders do 90% of the structural division work; shadows merely signal slight interactive affordance on hover or modal presentation.

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary balances modern software precision with approachable polish. Corners are clearly rounded without feeling bubbly or toy-like.

### Token Application

- **Standard Controls (`rounded-md` / 6px to 8px):** Buttons, text inputs, dropdown triggers, and interactive table badges.
- **Container Cards (`rounded-xl` / 12px to 14px):** Primary dashboard widgets, KPI cards, table enclosures, and modal dialogs.
- **Floating Panels & Outer Dialogs (`rounded-2xl` / 16px):** Large overlay surfaces and onboarding drawers.
- **Pills (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Status indicators, spending category tags, active navigation pills, and circular action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary:** Solid `#0F766E` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-lg` (8px). Hover: `#0D5C58`. Height: 36px (default), 44px (large). Font: `body-md` (medium 500). Focus: 2px offset ring with `#0F766E`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` text. Hover: `#F8FAFC` background with `#CBD5E1` border.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, `#475569` text. Hover: `#F1F5F9` background, `#0F172A` text.
- **Destructive:** Light wash `#FEF2F2`, `#DC2626` text, `1px solid #FEE2E2` border. Active/Hover: `#DC2626` solid with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Inputs & Selectors

- **Text Inputs:** Height 38px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, border-radius 8px. Text is `body-md`. Focus state uses border `#0F766E` and a 1px subtle ring `rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.15)`.
- **Numeric / Currency Inputs:** Left-aligned currency glyph (`$`) in muted slate, trailing font styled in `mono-val` with `tabular-nums`.

### Cards & KPI Tiles

- Wrapped in `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `rounded-xl` (12px), background `#FFFFFF`, padding `1.5rem` (`space-lg`).
- **KPI Card Structure:** Top row contains micro-label (`label-md` uppercase) and contextual action/icon. Center row showcases primary balance or metric using `mono-kpi` font. Bottom row holds trend delta pill with directional icon and comparison timeframe.

### Sidebar Navigation

- Vertical stack of list items (height 38px, `rounded-lg`, padding `0.5rem 0.75rem`).
- **Inactive:** `#475569` text and icon. Hover: `#F1F5F9` background, `#0F172A` text.
- **Active State:** `#F0FDFA` background, `#0F766E` text and icon, with an optional 3px vertical pill bar on the left edge.

### Status Chips & Badges

- Compact height (22px), `rounded-full`, horizontal padding 8px, font `label-sm`.
- **Surplus / Positive:** Background `#DCFCE7`, text `#15803D`.
- **Approaching Limit:** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`.
- **Breach / Alert:** Background `#FEE2E2`, text `#B91C1C`.
- **Neutral / Pending:** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`.

### Data Tables & Transaction Ledgers

- Row height: 48px. Header height: 36px with uppercase `label-sm` text.
- Subtle `1px solid #F1F5F9` row dividers. Alternating row fills are avoided; hover displays a light `#F8FAFC` highlight across the entire row.
- Currency amounts strictly right-aligned with `mono-val` typography.

### Advisory Alert / Cue Callout

- Special component for proactive tips. Framed in a light teal gradient boundary: `1px solid #CCFBF1`, background `#F0FDFA`, padding `1rem`. Incorporates an icon marker and actionable link inline.
